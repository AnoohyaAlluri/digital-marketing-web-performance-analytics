# Future Analytics Roadmap

The analytics roadmap follows measurement maturity.

## Stage 1 --- Reliable Measurement

Event definitions, source governance, lead validation, consistent
reporting windows.

## Stage 2 --- Diagnostic Analytics

Funnel analysis, source performance, website behavior, paid-media
comparisons, segmentation.

## Stage 3 --- Stronger Outcome Linkage

Reliable lifecycle stages and downstream business outcomes.

## Stage 4 --- Statistical Learning

Confidence intervals, cohort analysis, categorical tests, trend
analysis, experimentation.

## Stage 5 --- Predictive Analytics

Only after sufficient reliable outcomes exist: logistic regression,
conversion propensity, lead prioritization, or channel-quality modeling.

Predictive modeling is a future extension---not a claimed completed
capability of the current project.
