# Findings & Limitations

This file is intentionally structured as a reporting template. Public
findings will be populated only from synthetic or approved data.

## Reporting Format

### Finding

What the data shows.

### Interpretation

What the pattern may indicate.

### Limitation

What the evidence does not establish.

### Decision Relevance

Why the finding matters.

### Recommended Next Step

What should be investigated, tested, or measured next.

## Core Limitations to Monitor

-   Incomplete closed-loop lifecycle tracking
-   Platform attribution differences
-   Self-reported source ambiguity
-   Partial reporting periods
-   Tracking/configuration changes
-   Small segment sample sizes
-   Missing downstream outcomes
-   Duplicate or repeated inquiries
-   Changes in campaign mix
-   Correlation-versus-causation risk
