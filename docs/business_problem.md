# Business Problem

## Context

Digital marketing performance cannot be evaluated reliably by looking at
traffic, clicks, form submissions, or advertising metrics in isolation.

A prospect can move through multiple stages:

**Digital Discovery → Website Visit → Engagement → High-Intent Action →
Inquiry → Validated Lead → Qualification → Available Downstream Business
Outcome**

Each stage can be measured differently, and the available evidence may
come from multiple web analytics, advertising, lead-capture, and
operational systems.

## Core Business Question

> **How is digital acquisition and website performance changing, where
> are prospects progressing or dropping out of the funnel, and what
> opportunities exist to improve marketing performance, conversion,
> measurement, and lead progression?**

## Recurring Analysis Cycle

This project represents a recurring digital performance review conducted
approximately every **3--4 months**.

Each cycle evaluates the latest available evidence across acquisition,
website behavior, conversion, lead quality, funnel progression,
available downstream outcomes, attribution, statistical patterns, and
growth opportunities.

The objective is to answer:

1.  What changed?
2.  Where did it change?
3.  Which segments, channels, pages, or funnel stages contributed?
4.  What can the available evidence reliably explain?
5.  What cannot yet be concluded?
6.  Where are the largest measurement or conversion gaps?
7.  What should be investigated or tested next?
8.  What should be measured during the next cycle?

## Decision Support

The analysis can support digital marketing optimization, website
improvements, CRO, campaign evaluation, lead-quality improvement, funnel
optimization, tracking improvements, follow-up processes,
experimentation priorities, and growth planning.

## Analytical Principle

The project separates **Observation**, **Analysis**, **Limitation**,
**Recommendation**, and **Future Measurement**.

Correlation, platform-reported conversion, self-reported source, and
form submission are not automatically treated as proof of causal
marketing impact or completed business outcomes.
