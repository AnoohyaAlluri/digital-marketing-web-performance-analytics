# Attribution Framework

Attribution is treated as an evidence problem rather than a single
platform field.

## Evidence Types

### Platform Attribution

Conversions and source information reported by analytics or advertising
platforms.

### Self-Reported Source

How a prospect says they discovered the business.

### Observed Digital Evidence

UTMs, referrers, landing pages, event data, campaign metadata, or other
trackable signals.

### Downstream Evidence

Later business activity that can be linked with sufficient confidence.

## Key Guardrails

-   "Google Search" does not automatically mean "Google Ads."
-   A click does not automatically equal an inquiry.
-   An inquiry does not automatically equal a qualified lead.
-   A downstream identity match does not automatically prove campaign
    causality.
-   ROI requires reliable cost, attribution, outcome, and value
    definitions.

## Goal

The goal is to improve attribution maturity while making clear what is
known, what is inferred, and what remains unmeasured.
