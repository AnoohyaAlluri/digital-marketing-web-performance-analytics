# Statistical Analysis Framework

Statistical analysis is included to improve interpretation of recurring
digital performance---not to create artificial sophistication.

## Analysis Types

### Descriptive Statistics

Counts, rates, distributions, medians, averages, proportions,
variability, and segment profiles.

### Period-over-Period Analysis

Compares recurring reporting windows and quantifies absolute and
relative changes.

### Conversion-Rate Analysis

Evaluates progression between defined funnel stages where denominators
are reliable.

### Confidence Intervals

Adds uncertainty ranges around rates or proportions when appropriate.

### Segment / Cohort Analysis

Compares channels, lead groups, time periods, opportunity
characteristics, or other defensible segments.

### Correlation Analysis

Explores relationships among traffic, spend, engagement, demand, and
outcomes without claiming causality.

### Categorical Tests

Chi-square may be used when assumptions are satisfied. Fisher's Exact
Test may be more appropriate for small categorical samples.

### Trend Analysis

Examines whether observed movement persists across repeated cycles.

### Regression --- Future Extension

Regression or propensity modeling should only be introduced after
sufficient reliable historical outcomes exist.

## Required Checks

Before statistical testing: - Confirm definitions and denominators. -
Confirm the analysis period. - Check missingness and duplicates. - Check
sample size. - Check test assumptions. - Separate exploratory findings
from confirmatory conclusions. - Document limitations.

## Reporting Standard

Every statistical result should state: **Question → Method → Result →
Interpretation → Limitation → Business relevance.**
